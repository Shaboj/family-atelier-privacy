Family Atelier Child Safety Standards

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Family Atelier is a private app for families. Families are invite only. There is no public feed and no way to search for strangers. People must be 13 or older to use the app.

Our position

We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). Any such content or behavior is banned in Family Atelier.

What we do

1. Private by design: only people invited to a family can see that family's posts, chats and calls.

2. Reports: users can report content or people that worry them. We review every report about child safety first.

3. Action: we remove violating content and ban the accounts involved.

4. Legal reporting: we report confirmed CSAM to the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC) and to the proper law enforcement, as the law requires.

5. Compliance: we follow Google Play policies and the child safety laws that apply to us.

Contact

For child safety questions or to report a concern, email gameoncreation@gmail.com. This contact can speak about our CSAM prevention practices and compliance.
