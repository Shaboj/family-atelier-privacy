Family Atelier Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Family Atelier is a private app for families. This page explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We wrote it in plain words.

Who we are

Family Atelier is made by an independent developer. For any privacy question write to gameoncreation@gmail.com.

Who can use the app

You must be 13 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you think a child under 13 has an account, write to us and we will delete it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and profile photo from Google Sign-In.
  • Family content: posts, photos, videos, messages, comments, reactions and call information that you and your family members create inside your family spaces.
  • Family membership: which families you own or joined, and your role in them.
  • Device and notification data: a push notification token so we can send you alerts about family activity and calls.
  • Purchase data: if you buy a plan or an extra family, Google Play gives us a purchase token and the product you bought. We never see your card number.
  • Basic technical data: app version and crash or error information needed to keep the app working.

How we use it

  • To run the app: sign you in, show your family feed, chat and calls.
  • To send notifications you need, such as a new message or an incoming call.
  • To check purchases and handle refunds.
  • To keep the app secure and fix problems.

We do not sell your data. We do not show ads. We do not use your content to build advertising profiles.

Who can see your content

Only members of the family space where you share something can see it. Photos and videos are stored encrypted. We do not share your content with other families or the public.

Services we use

The app relies on trusted providers that process data for us: Google Firebase (sign-in, database, notifications), Google Play (billing and distribution), and Cloudflare (a relay that helps with calls, purchase checks and file transfer). Each provider has its own privacy policy.

How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account is active. Purchase records are kept as long as needed for refunds and legal reasons.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • You can edit or delete your own posts and messages inside the app.
  • You can leave a family at any time.
  • To delete your account and the personal data linked to it, email gameoncreation@gmail.com from the email address you used to sign in, with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete it within 30 days.

Security

We use encryption for media, secure connections, and access rules so that only family members can read family content. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. If a change is important, we will tell you in the app.
